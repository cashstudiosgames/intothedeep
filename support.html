How to play

Tap Start Dive. Swipe left or right to move between the three lanes, collect gems, and dodge hazards. Your oxygen drains the whole time. Swipe up to surface and bank your gems. Make sure you leave enough oxygen for the trip back, because gems are only banked if you reach the surface.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app removes it, and it is not backed up to any account of ours.

How do I turn the sound off?
Tap the Sound button at the top right of the home screen.

How do I unlock new levels?
Bank gems over time. Tap the level button on the home screen to see how many lifetime gems each level needs.
