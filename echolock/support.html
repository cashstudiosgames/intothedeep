How to play

Swipe to move and reach the glowing exit. Tap Echo to end your run: the level resets and an echo of you replays every move you just made, right beside you, then stands still where it finished. Use your echoes to hold plates, open doors, flip switches and push crates while you go somewhere else. The Tutorial teaches everything in eight short levels.

Frequently asked questions

What does Paradox mean?
Your move would break the timeline, for example by blocking an echo's path or closing a door on someone, so it is not allowed. Try a different move or tap Wait.

What is the Goal?
The fewest moves the level can be solved in. Match it and the level turns gold.

I am stuck. Can I get help?
Tap Hint. Each hint shows the route for one run, starting with your first echo.

How do I unlock chapters?
Finish every level of the Tutorial to open Chapter 1, and every level of a chapter to open the next.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app removes it, and it is not backed up to any account of ours.
