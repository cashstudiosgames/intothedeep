Into the Deep Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026. Published by Cash Studios.

Into the Deep is a game that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what happens with your information. The short version: we do not collect it.

Information we collect

None. Into the Deep does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign in, and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (such as your gems, upgrades, items, unlocked levels, and your sound setting) in a small file on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This file stays on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this file.

Advertising, tracking, and third parties

Into the Deep contains no ads, no tracking, and no third party analytics or advertising services. We do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

Internet access

The game does not need an internet connection and does not use one.

Children

Because we collect no information from anyone, the game is safe for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cashstudios.games@gmail.com.
